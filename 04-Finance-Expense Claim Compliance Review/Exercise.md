# Question #04: Cost reimbursement review compliance Nuclear

# Background

The financial department of an enterprise is required to review a large number of claims per month. Manual review requires matching the type of costs, system limits, invoice amounts, approval of materials, duplicate declarations and remaining departmental budgets. Available data includes normal claims, inconsistent amounts, duplicate invoices, excesses, lack of prior approval and budget excess.

You are a member of the Financial Process Optimization Project, and you need to build a cost reimbursement clearance smart body, give retroactive results for each record, review results and divert unusual applications to follow-up, including partial or manual review.

# Provide material

- `Reimbursement forms.csv ': 36 claims records, including invoice numbers, approval numbers and the terms of reference for business matters.
- `Reimbursable system.txt ': reimbursable range, single limit, duplicate filing, approval and budgetary rules...
- `Sectoral budget.csv ': Monthly budget for six sectors, approved expenditures and residual amounts...

# Business tasks to be accomplished

1. Processing of all 36 claims, checking the type of costs, amounts declared and invoiced, single limits, prior approval number, duplicate declarations and remaining departmental budgets
2. State of audit of each record export, hit-and-fall rule, terms of the system, evidence fields, reimbursable amounts and recommended trails...
3. Generate a summary of audits, including at least the number of compliance notes, the number of unusual entries, the distribution of unusual types, the amounts declared by departments and the excess budget
4. Draft audit opinions on unusual claims, clearly distinguishing between "returning additional material", "partly through "and "transferred manual review" within the limits".

# Unusual and border

- Duplicate claims should give comparable evidence of the original record and should not be based solely on a similar text.
- Over-standard but system-permissible costs of review require the calculation of the reimbursable amount and the basis of the calculation...
- Records exceeding the remaining departmental budget cannot be automatically passed, and should be subject to review by the head of department and the financial BP.
- "Unusual" does not amount to "fraud." The information is not available to be filled out, and the applicant's motive cannot be inferred.
- The system or budget should be updated to allow for a review and retention of the results and retroactive of the original claims.

# Delivery requirements

The results of the different situations, such as normal performance, inconsistent amounts, duplication, over-charter, lack of approval and over-budget... The manner of realization is designed by the athletes themselves.
