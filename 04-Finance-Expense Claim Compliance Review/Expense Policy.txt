System number: ZX-FIN-012
Version: V4.0
Period of application: 2026-07-01 to 2026-12-31

Article 1
Article 2. Invoices must be in the same amount as declared; the same invoice number, business, date and amount combination may not be repeated.
Article 3 Single control standards: $800 for city transport, $650 for accommodation/night, $150 for travel meals/person/day, $2,000 for office supplies and $3,000 for training. Only the standard amount is subject to review when the standard is exceeded.
Article 4
Article 5. Where the amount declared exceeds the remaining budget of the department, it shall be subject to manual review by the head of department and the financial BP, which shall not be automatically passed...
Article 6. Shortfalls, discrepancies in amounts or insufficient approval material shall be identified as material to be filled; "unusual" shall not be equated with fraud.
