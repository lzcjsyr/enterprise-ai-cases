Document: Standards for internal control of cereals products from oat nuts and nuts
Number: QS-GBAR-02
Version: V2.0
Entry into force: 2026-07-01
Status: Active at present

1. Sensor: whole, smouldered, non-stereotyped, non-obsessed, non-halaic... any non-conformity with the summary project.
Net content: Single mark 35g, 10 average net content not less than 35.0g, single not less than 33.5g.
3. Water: not more than 12.0 g/ 100g.
4. Acid prices (in fat): not more than 5.0 mg/g.
5. Peroxide (fat): not more than 0.25 g/ 100g.
Total bacterial fallout: 5 parallel samples, m=10,000 CFU/g, M=100,000 CFU/g, with a maximum of 2 samples allowed between m and M, and no sample exceeding M.
7. Entire enteric strains: 5 parallel samples, m=10 CFU/g, M=100 CFU/g, with a maximum of 2 samples allowed between m and M, and no sample exceeding M.
8. Salmonella: 5 25g samples are not detectable.
