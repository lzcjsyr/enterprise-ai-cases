# Question 06: Smart extract from contract payment terms

# Background

An enterprise procurement department needs to have quick access to payment nodes, collateral and liability for breach of contract at the contract approval and financial planning stages... Different contracts are structured and presented differently, and the same field may be scattered over multiple terms, such as contract amounts, payment modalities, acceptance and liability for breach of contract, and may not be expressly agreed upon.

You are a member of the Digital Project for the Review of Procurement Contracts, and you need to build a smart body for the extracting of contractual payment clauses that provides a retrospective structured review of procurement, finance and legal matters without relying on industry practice to supplement it.

# Provide material

- `contracts/`: 3 text-type PDF procurement or service contracts with different payment structures...
- `Dicking field description.txt ': fields to be extracted, confidence calibre, evidentiary requirements and method of verifying amounts...

# Business tasks to be accomplished

1. Processing of all three contracts, identifying contract numbers and names, including total taxes, currencies, rates of taxation, advances, milestone payment nodes, plethora of bonds, pre-payment materials and default terms...
2. Output ratio for each payment node, amount, trigger condition and payment period; statement of default applicable to the person to whom the statement applies, base figure, date rate and cumulative ceiling...
3. Each field shall be supported by proof of its original language not exceeding 120 words, and shall be marked as high, medium or low in confidence in accordance with the rules.
4. Verify whether the sum of the payment nodes and the Quality Assurances equals the total contract amount and whether the sum of the percentages is 100 per cent, with a separate statement of the difference, if rounded.
5. Generate horizontal comparisons of the three contracts, payment time lines and risk review lists.

# Unusual and border

- Fields not agreed in the contract must be exported "no found" and may not be added to other contracts or trade usages.
- When there is a need to calculate or to present a disagreement across the articles, confidence should be reduced and evidence of the calculation process or conflict should be included.
- In the case of low confidence, unequal amounts, clauses that are clearly unfavourable to our side or unclear legal liability, creating points for procurement review, negotiating inquiries and legal confirmation matters...
- The replacement of contract documents should allow for duplication and should not entrench the concrete results of the three examples in progress.

# Delivery requirements

On-site demonstrations are required to demonstrate the treatment of clear terms, cross-section calculations, field missing and adverse terms... the manner of realization is designed by the player himself.
