The following fields are to be drawn from each contract:
1. Contract number and contract name
Total amount of tax contract, currency, rate of tax
3. Proportion of advance payments, amounts, trigger conditions
4. Milestone payment nodes: ratio, amount, trigger terms and payment period
5. Proportion of Quality Bond, Amount, Conditions for Release
6. Prepayment materials: invoices, acceptance orders, statements, etc.
7. Default clauses: persons to whom they apply, base figure, day ratio and ceiling
8. Evidence of original language: clause and not more than 120 words of original language
9. Confidence: High = clause clear and amounts verifiable; medium = calculation required across clause or ambiguity; low = no found or conflicting.

(a) Currency verification: the sum of the payment nodes and the Quality Guarantee should be equal to the total contract amount; The total ratio should be 100 per cent. Separately if the difference is rounded.
Missing fields must be exported "unfinished" and cannot be supplemented according to industry practice.
