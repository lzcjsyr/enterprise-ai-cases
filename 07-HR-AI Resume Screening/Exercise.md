# Question 01: Screening and Matching of Smart Curricula vitae

# Background

An enterprise is recruiting "Ai Product Manager (Enterprise Smart Directions). HR has received 30 candidate profiles, which contain both PDF and Word formats. The candidates ' background varies considerably, ranging from highly matched to under-age, competency-based sections, missing critical information or insufficient evidence of project results.

You are a member of the HR Department ' s Smart Project, which requires the construction of a reusable CVA assessment smart body to help HR quickly complete the initial screening and continue to receive the results of the screenings into the interview preparation section. The smart body provides only supporting decisions and cannot substitute HR for final recruitment decisions.

# Provide material

- `Performance JD.txt ': Job assignment, duties, requirements for appointment, priority conditions and rules against bias...
- `Assessment dimensions description.txt ': skills, experience, academic and basic competencies, quality of evidence and ability to collaborate.
- `resumes/`: 30 dissensitized curricula vitae.
- `resumes.zip ': uploading of a single document consistent with the contents of the curriculum vitae.

# Business tasks to be accomplished

1. Processing of all 30 CVs, identifying key information such as Xing Ming, academic qualifications, professional, work-life, B-end product experience, AI-related experience, core skills, project results and evidence.
2. A person-by-person assessment, strictly based on the JD and the evaluation rules, showing the original division of four dimensions, the weighting points, the total score and the recommended conclusion. All points should be traced back to the CV evidence or to the clear rules.
3. Generate a list of recommendations for candidates in total order, including at least ranking, Xing Ming, score of dimensions, total, recommendations, core advantages, key panels and summaries of evidence.
4. To generate interview focus, follow-up questions and interview evaluation records for candidates recommended for interview, matching their short-board and pending clearances.

# Unusual and border

- The information not provided in the curriculum vitae must be marked "to be verified" and cannot be inferred from it.
- The absence of baselines, statistical cycles or quantitative results of individual duties should reduce the intensity of evidence and list interview clearances, and should not be directly exaggerated or falsified...
- No reduction in the amount of information not related to the capacity to perform a post, such as age, sex, origin, marriage and birth, or the level of a post.
- Support the re-evaluation of JDs or changes in scoring weights, and describe changes in version and ranking...

# Delivery requirements

On-site demonstrations are required to demonstrate the full process of preparing personal assessments and interviews from the entry of curricula vitae to the sorting results... in a manner that is achieved using components and workflow structures designed by the players themselves.
