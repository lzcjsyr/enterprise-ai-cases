Examination question 01: Smart CV screening and matching assessment

Background
Recruitment positions, evaluation rules and candidate experience match each other. Materials include genuine screenings such as strong matching, competency bias, lack of years, missing critical information and insufficient evidence.

List of documents
- `Performance JD.txt ': Job duties, job requirements and rules against bias
- `Assessment dimensions description.txt ': 4-D scoring calibration, weights, inter-conclusive and abnormal treatment
- `resumes/`: 30 biographical notes of dissensitive candidates in a mixed PDF/Word format
- `resumes.zip ': uploading a single file package consistent with the contents of the resumes directory, generated after conversion

Description of use
1. All persons, enterprises and numbers have been re-intensived and not correspond to the actual person or enterprise.
2. Data retain field relationships in real business, time sequence, monetary constraints and abnormal patterns
3. Harmonization of documentation UTF-8Encoding; CSV first line is field name, and the identification category field should be processed as text.
4. No one may use the verification information in the "The Probator Retains" catalogue.
