A practical end-to-end enterprise AI workflow

Five steps.

01 · Define the request

Explain the goal and required fields; ask AI to propose a cleaning plan.

02 · Clean the data

Standardize fields and formats, remove duplicates, and add missing dimensions.

03 · Verify with Qichacha

Check business registration details and fill missing fields using Qichacha.

04 · Create one source of truth

Export a single Excel file as the trusted source.

05 · Add it to the knowledge base

Upload it to Lexiang, build the index, and enable ongoing Q&A.

Key principle:Decide what users will ask, then work backward to define the required fields.Answer quality depends on field design. Uploading dirty data spreads incorrect answers.

Step One:
Describe the goal and ask AI for a data-cleaning plan.

I have Excel company lists from seven business parks, with inconsistent fields. Goal: merge them into one Excel file, upload it to a company knowledge base, and support routine questions such as “Which companies are in this district?”, “Which companies are in this industry?”, and “Which companies are located in this park?” First propose a data-cleaning plan. It must: 1) define a standard field list and map the source fields to it; 2) inventory all differences and data-quality issues across the seven files; and 3) give a handling rule for each issue.
IssueExampleHandling rule
Inconsistent fieldsFiles have 11–15 columns; examples include “registered funds” vs. “registered capital” and “industry sector” vs. “industry subcategory.”Map all source fields to 21 standard fields.
Formatting issuesMany tab characters; registered capital appears as “RMB 3,000,000,” “RMB 500,000,” or “500 (in units of RMB 10,000).”Convert amounts to numeric values in units of RMB 10,000.
Mixed formatsSocial-insurance headcount appears as “1 (2024)”—a count followed by a year.Extract the numeric count.
Duplicate recordsYunxi Valley appears in two files with 591 companies in each; the lists fully overlap.Deduplicate by company name.
Irregular layoutThe Fuxi Industrial Zone headers start on row 2, and the fields differ from the other files.Parse this file separately.
Missing key fieldsCredit codes are missing for 34% of companies; none of the files has a park or district field.Add the missing location field and verify records with Qichacha.

Step Four:
Test routine questions and answers.

Once the knowledge base is ready, ask realistic questions to check whether the AI can retrieve the company data correctly.

Which companies related to integrated circuits are in Xiangzhou District?

17 matches, including Shimin Semiconductor Technology, OnePalm Digital Technology, Zhuhai Hutong Microelectronics, and Zhuhai Shuangtan Technology.

Which companies are located in Yunxi Valley?

591 companies in the Yunxi Valley list, including Shanghai Jiao Tong University (Zhuhai) Intelligent Technology.

Which companies in the High-Tech Zone have registered capital above RMB 10 million?

Filter by “District = High-Tech Zone” and “Registered Capital > 1,000” (in units of RMB 10,000), then return the matching companies.

How many companies are in Meixing Industrial Park? Is this company still operating?

678 companies; Qichacha verification supports the registration-status field.

Practice applying the workflow:This workflow applies to any “multiple spreadsheets → knowledge base” task, such as customer lists, supplier records, or fixed-asset lists. Before starting, ask AI to identify the target fields, deduplication key, external verification source, and results that require human review.
