Turn repetitive categorization into a reviewable workflow

Purchase Details
Automated Categorization and Labeling

Task: Read the purchase details, apply the accounting categories, and write the results to a new Excel file. Then summarize the categorized purchases in a chart.

Materials: Cafeteria April Purchase Details.xls · delivery-xlsx skill · frontend-design skill

Page 2 of 5

Follow the steps:
A four-step workflow.

01 · Read

Identify the headers, item-name column, and source layout.

02 · Understand

Read the categorization rules and examples in the skill.

03 · Categorize

Match each item to a category by its meaning, not just by keywords.

04 · Write Back

Add category codes and names to a new Excel file, then review the results manually.

Theskills/delivery-xlsxfolder contains this case’s operating guide. Have the AI use it; don’t ask it to guess or change the table structure.
Page 3 of 5

Step One:
Create a reviewable categorized table.

Use the skill in skills/delivery-xlsx in the project root to categorize the items in Cafeteria April Purchase Details.xls by accounting category.
When the AI is done, pause before the next step. Open Cafeteria April Purchases - Categorized.xlsx, verify the categories, and reconcile the amounts.
Page 4 of 5

Step Two:
Turn the table into a chart.

Use Cafeteria April Purchases - Categorized.xlsx to create a second deliverable: summarize the amounts by ingredient category and save Cafeteria April Purchase Summary.xlsx. Also create Cafeteria April Purchase Analysis.html with a pie chart showing each category’s share of total spend. Keep the design clean and suitable for a business report. Open the HTML when finished and check the chart, amounts, percentages, and English labels.
Page 5 of 5

Practice applying the workflow:
Use your own detail table.

Try expense claims, inventory, or order details. Before starting, ask the AI to identify the field to categorize, the source of the rules, and which results require human review.

Done means more than “a file was generated”: each category has a reason, amounts reconcile, unknown items are flagged, and the summary chart is clear. Do not send real files containing sensitive data to an uncontrolled external service.
