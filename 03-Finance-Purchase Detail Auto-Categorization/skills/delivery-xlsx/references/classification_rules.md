# The principles of classification and judgement

- Sort by nature and by context, not just a single keyword.
- Brands, place of origin, specifications, weight, head count, processing and packaging methods are usually acquis decorative information without changing the nature of the commodity.
- Meat, seafood, eggs and meat products are classified as veggies; vegetables, beans, mushrooms, pickles and dried products are classified as vegetable accelerants.
- The rice flour and common paste staples are classified as staple foods; the oil and salt sauce, the spices, sugar and the compound spices are classified as food and oil.
- Milk, coconuts, juice, soy milk and drinks, among other things.
- Food bags, boxes and film-covering items directly related to food packaging are classified as packing groups.
- Non-food consumables such as napkins, gloves, cleaning supplies, cookers and aprons are classified as low-value and low-employable.
- When it is not possible to determine completely, select the closest category from this category 7 and record it in the review list, without creating "other" or "pending confirmation" subjects.
