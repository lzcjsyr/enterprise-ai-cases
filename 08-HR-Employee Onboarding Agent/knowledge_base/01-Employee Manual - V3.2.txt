Department of Human Resources

Chapter I General provisions
Article 1.1. This manual applies to employees who have entered into labour relations with a company... in the event of conflict with the system, in the latest valid version of the special regime...
Article 1.2. New employees shall complete the physical information, emergency contact and bank card information within five working days of entry on duty.

Chapter II. Admission and probationary service
Article 2.1. The objectives of the probationary period are confirmed by the immediate manager within seven natural days of entry into office.
Article 2.2. Retransmissions shall be initiated 10 working days before the end of the probationary period and shall not imply automatic adoption...

Chapter III. Working behaviour
Section 3.1. Office information is classified as "public, internal, sensitive, strictly sensitive".
Article 3.2. Client data may not be uploaded to personal disks or AI services without company approval.

Chapter IV Separation and handover
Article 4.1. Separation from service shall be subject to the completion of the transfer of assets, accounts, documents and in-service matters.
