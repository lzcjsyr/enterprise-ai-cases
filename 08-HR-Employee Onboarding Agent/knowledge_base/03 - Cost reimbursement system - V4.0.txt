Document symbol: ZX-FIN-012
Version: V4.0
Entry into force: 2026-06-15
Department of Finance

Article 2.1 Reimbursement shall be submitted within 30 natural days of the cost incurred and by 10 January of the following year at the latest...
Article 2.2. Reheading of invoices and tax numbers must be consistent with company information on the issuance of tickets; electronic invoices should be verified.
Article 3.1. Client hospitality of more than $1,000 must be approved prior to receipt of the OO.
Regulation 3.2. Inland transportation shall state the place of departure and the cause of business; no reimbursement shall be made for commuting expenses...
Article 4.1 Financial payments are centralized on Fridays on Tuesdays and holidays are extended upon completion of approval.
Article 4.2. Status of reimbursement "financial review" is not equivalent to completion of payment.
