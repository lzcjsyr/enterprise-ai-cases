Department of Administration and Human Resources

Rule 2.1 Conference rooms are booked via the business calendar, with regular meetings available 14 days in advance and client meetings 30 days in advance.
Rule 2.2. Unsigned and unspecified documents for more than 15 minutes, the system automatically releases the meeting rooms.
Article 3.1. The daily meal allowance is paid at the rate of the salary for the actual day of attendance; the daily allowance is not repeated on the day of the mission.
Article 3.2. Payment of medical examination on entry on duty on the basis of a compliance invoice within 30 days of entry on duty, up to a maximum of $300.
Article 4.1 Social insurance and housing pension are governed by the main subject of the employment contract and current policy, with the individual ' s contribution base confirmed in HR writing.
