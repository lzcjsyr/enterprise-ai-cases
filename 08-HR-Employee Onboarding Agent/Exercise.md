# 02: New staff entry to the Practising Service

# Background

A medium-sized manufacturing enterprise has new staff on board every month. New employees may have a different application or old or new version of the system, including a system of probation, time and absence, reimbursement, IT account numbers and privileges, conference rooms, welfare, etc., and HR and IT help desks need to answer a large number of questions. The existing system documents have a symbol, version, effective date and article number.

You are a member of the Digitalization of Corporate Service (DIP) project, and you need to build a PLC smart body that can answer not only systemic questions, but also continue to receive in-service questions and answers, supplementing information or hand-held service transfer links.

# Provide material

- `knowledge base/ ': 5 enterprise systems documents covering employee manuals, time and absences, reimbursement of expenses, IT accounts and privileges, conference rooms and staff welfare...
- `List of frequently asked questions.csv ': 20 test questions containing direct answers, needing clarification, conflict of regime and situations in which a person should be transferred...

# Business tasks to be accomplished

1. Answers to new staff questions based on the availability of system documents, and the content of responses must be consistent with the original system.
2. Each reasoned answer indicates at least the name of the system, the version and the number of the article; the conditions of application, the processor, the time frame, the material required and the next steps, if required.
3. Retain the necessary context in the multi-cycle dialogue. Users should be able to continue to address the issue without repeating the entire issue, by the type of additional leave, date, post or entry stage.
4. Generate to-do lists based on the user ' s position or entry stage, and each to-do is linked to the corresponding system basis.

# Unusual and border

- When there is insufficient information on the issue, questions of targeted clarification should be raised, without any presumption of self-determination.
- Where there is a conflict between regimes, the location of the conflict should be specified, as judged by the effective date, the version and the scope of application; I can't judge the transfer.
- Where the knowledge base has no answers, it clearly states that "the existing system has no basis" and that no corporate rules can be supplemented by common sense or external information.
- Generate summary of transferable matters, including user claims, known information, detected systems and points to be confirmed, when HR, finance or IT is required to confirm.
- After the system is updated, the answer should reflect the current version or date of the update...

# Delivery requirements

On-site demonstrations are required to present the typical situations of direct question and answer, multiple rounds of clarification, non-rejection or transfer of labour, in-service generation, etc. The method of realization is designed by the player.
