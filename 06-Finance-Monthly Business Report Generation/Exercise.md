# Question #05: Monthly Business Data Bulletin

# Background

A chain retailing enterprise is required to submit a monthly business brief to the Management Committee. Management needs to understand the three levels of sales, the regional and the door shop, the Māori, the cost and the flow of customers, but manual aggregation and writing of reports can be subject to varying calculus, mistook or extrapolation of the reasons.

You're a smart project member of the financial analysis team, and you need to build a smart body that can process monthly data, recognize anomalies and generate management briefs.

# Provide material

- `Monthly operating data. csv ': data from 4 regions, 24 stores this month, last month and the same period last year.
- `Briefing template.txt ': Management reporting structure, indicator formulae, abnormal thresholds and evidentiary requirements...

# Business tasks to be accomplished

1. Processing data on all 24 stores, with a ratio of growth by required calibre, ring growth, Māori and cost rates, with 1 decimal place remaining.
2. The formation of corporate and regional aggregations, where the regional component is required to demonstrate sales, year-on-year, ring-by-year, Māori and cost rates, and rank them in terms of year-on-year growth rates...
3. Identify, as a rule of the template, those outlets whose sales decreased by more than 15 per cent each year, with a cost rate of more than 20 per cent or a gross gross domestic product of less than 25 per cent. Each anomaly must show the door-storage ID, the original amount, the calculation and the calculation results.
4. Generate a full monthly briefing note containing executive summaries, regional performance, list of door-shop anomalies and actions. The executive summary should not exceed 250 words, the list of actions should contain questions, evidence, reasons for the assumption, validation of movements, areas of responsibility and recommended duration.

# Unusual and border

- All reported figures must be derived from raw data or subject to review, and must not be estimated or completed by model...
- The denominator is 0-hour mark "uncalculable" and cannot be forced to output percentages.
- Reason analysis must be labelled as "assumption" and give the data or actions required for further validation, and the facts not provided may not be included in the report.
- The monthly data should be regenerated and show changes in the data period and in the report version.

# Delivery requirements

The final results should be suitable for management reading and sufficient computation detail for financial review. On-site demonstration data calculations, abnormal location and reporting generation.
